JWORKS 10.2.6 — REQUIRED WRANGLER CHANGE

Open the existing wrangler.jsonc in GitHub. DO NOT replace your database_id.

Inside the "assets" object change:

  "run_worker_first": true

to:

  "run_worker_first": ["/api/*"]

Recommended assets block shape:

  "assets": {
    "directory": "./public/",
    "binding": "ASSETS",
    "run_worker_first": ["/api/*"]
  },

Keep every other setting, especially the real D1 database_id, unchanged.
