BUILT BECAUSE I NEEDED IT

I wasn’t looking to build another project-management app.

I was managing real projects with schedules in one place, tasks somewhere else, spreadsheets tracking the gaps, meeting notes that had to become actions and reports that had to be rebuilt by hand. The tools that were supposed to help often became another thing to manage. So I started building the workspace I actually wanted to use. That became JWorks.

One question still drives it: would this actually make someone’s workday easier?
